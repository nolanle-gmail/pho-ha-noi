Staff app

pho-ha-noi-waitlist.fly.dev

For servers, hosts, bussers, kitchen — everyone on the floor.

  1. Open the link in Safari on your iPhone.Type pho-ha-noi-waitlist.fly.dev in Safari — not Chrome.
  2. Add it to your Home Screen.Tap Share (the box with an ↑) → Add to Home Screen → Add.
  3. Open the app from the new Home Screen icon named PHN - Staff.This is the important part — notifications only work from the installed icon, never a Safari tab.
  4. Sign in with your 10-digit phone number and password.
  5. Go to ⚙️ Settings → 📲 Device notifications and tap Enable notifications.The app's buttons are in English.
  6. Tap Allow on the iPhone pop-up.🔔 On for this device
🖥️

Management app

pho-ha-noi-management.fly.dev

For managers, general managers, admins and the owner.

  1. On a phone: open the link in Safari, then Share → Add to Home Screen and open it from the icon.On a computer you can skip this — just use it in your browser.
  2. Sign in to the console.
  3. Open Account Settings from your name at the top-right.
  4. Under 📲 Device notifications, tap Enable notifications → Allow.You'll get messages, chat, and any floor alerts sent to you.

Management app

Good to know

  • Set it up on each device you use — your phone, and your tablet or computer are separate.
  • Already had the app installed from before? Delete that icon and add it again, so it loads the latest version with the notifications setting.
  • Sound & vibration follow your phone's own notification settings for the app (on iPhone, also the silent switch).
  • Tapping a notification opens the app straight to the message, chat, or alert.
  • To turn it off: same screen → Turn off, or in your phone's notification settings for the app.

SMS Messaging Terms & Privacy

Last updated: September 8, 2026

This policy explains the text-message (SMS) program operated by HaNoi Bistros, doing business as Phở Hà Nội ("we," "us"), how you consent to it, and how we handle your mobile information.

1. Program & message types

We send transactional text messages to people who opt in, in two programs:

  • Guest waitlist updates — a confirmation when you join a restaurant's waiting list, and a notification when your table is ready.
  • Staff operational notifications — for employees only: shift and break reminders, team messages, and urgent floor alerts.

We do not send marketing or promotional texts through this program.

2. How you opt in (consent)

  • Guests opt in by checking the SMS consent box when joining the waitlist at our check-in page. The box is not pre-checked, and providing your number is optional — you can still be seated without it.
  • Staff opt in during onboarding by providing a mobile number and agreeing to receive operational notifications. Consent is not a condition of employment.

3. Message frequency

Message frequency varies by activity — typically 1–3 messages per restaurant visit for guests, and up to roughly 10 messages per working day for staff on shift.

4. Cost

Message and data rates may apply. We do not charge for the messages themselves; your mobile carrier's standard rates apply.

5. Opt out and get help

STOP — Reply STOP to any message to unsubscribe. You will receive one confirmation and then no further messages. Reply START to opt back in.

HELP — Reply HELP for help, or contact us using the details in section 8.

6. Your privacy & mobile information

For this SMS program we collect only what we need to send the messages above: your mobile phone number, your name, and — for staff — your work schedule and role. We use this information solely to deliver the waitlist and operational messages you opted into.

We do not sell your information, and we do not share your mobile number or SMS consent with third parties for their own marketing. Mobile information is shared only with the messaging provider that delivers the texts on our behalf, strictly to send these messages.

You may request that we delete your mobile number from this program at any time — reply STOP, or contact us (section 8).

7. Carrier disclaimer

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Message delivery is subject to your carrier's network and is not guaranteed.

8. Contact us

Questions about this SMS program? Email harry@phohanoi.com.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy; the "Last updated" date above reflects the current version.
